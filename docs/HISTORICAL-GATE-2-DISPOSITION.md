Its recorded prototype coverage and gaps describe that earlier snapshot and cannot establish current coverage after subsequent authorized prototype work. It explicitly does not accept D-100 or Checkpoint 11 and does not establish physical-device, actual zoom, assistive-technology or full accessibility conformance. No present browser or usability review has been conducted here.

The screen documents also contain historical statements that predate D-094/D-095 and the effective v0.9 amendment; later approved account and six-purpose email provisions take precedence within their recorded scope. Current prototype content, journey completeness, review findings, accessibility design evidence and any dependencies therefore need an appropriately scoped fresh evidence reconciliation before a claim that the D-093 package has been reviewed. Prototype simulations establish neither actual authentication, authorization, booking, email delivery nor product acceptance.

## D-079, Q-008 and continuing controls

D-079 retains substantive policy questions while D-098 changes the timing model for routine future work. Before affected security/privacy review, resolve applicable retention/deletion, exceptional operator-access duration and break-glass policy; before release/deployment approval, resolve applicable printed-roster handling/disposal. D-091 distinguishes a synthetic restore from verification of a particular real client's backup: protected backups and tested restoration precede operational reliance, and a separately authorized privacy-safe real-backup verification approach remains necessary. Neither a historical architecture disposition nor a prototype review constitutes that verification. Actual custody, support, access, backup, incident and restoration responsibilities must be allocated for each installation; infrastructure custody does not confer client-record access.

Qualified security, privacy/legal, accessibility and data-integrity reviews, applicable automated and human verification, genuine product-fit review, and separate release/deployment and operational approvals remain independently applicable. PH-01–PH-10 and actual owners remain open as recorded in the [specialist handoff](technical/ARCH-EVIDENCE-REVIEW.md); their exact milestones differ. A historical disposition cannot silently accept unresolved critical security or integrity defects or replace the required independent review.

# TECH-WP-02 — Track B: Identity and security candidate evaluation

> **Evaluation draft — 2026-11-13.** This comparison advances [TECH-WP-01 Track B](TECH-WP-01-IDENTITY.md); it selects or approves no technology, policy, proof, vendor, package, role assignment, recovery procedure or implementation. No application test, privacy/security/accessibility assessment or operational trial has been performed. Recommendations are conditional inputs for independent specialist and Product Owner review.

## Governing constraints and decision context

[NFR-003 v0.9 and AC-027–029](../requirements/REQUIREMENTS-SPECIFICATION-v0.9.md) are effective with v0.8/v0.7. Every authenticated internal user must complete MFA before protected access, including authorized internal operators; accessible, individually attributable paths must exist without a personal device. Guest and customer-facing accounts are outside that internal MFA coverage. Existing guest appointment access nevertheless requires independent appointment-scoped original-booker proof; email, reference, QR, personal-data match or account possession alone is insufficient. D-103, D-104, D-105 and D-108 in the [Decision Register](../decisions/DECISION_REGISTER.md) retain their scopes: default-deny by actor/service/action, no self-elevation, independently authorized additional-super-admin appointment and sole-admin recovery, distinct booker/attendee/representative authority, narrow contested holds, and no standing operator access to client business data. D-104 does not select legal roles, data fields, retention or providers. D-095 governs email purposes.

[SDD-002 v0.5](../specs/SDD-002-INTERNAL-IDENTITY-PROVISIONING-AND-PERMISSION-ENFORCEMENT.md), [SDD-008 v0.2](../specs/SDD-008-APPOINTMENT-SCOPED-GUEST-ACCESS-AND-ASSISTED-RECOVERY.md), and [SDD-013 v0.3](../specs/SDD-013-CONTESTED-ACTION-HOLDS-AND-ASSIGNED-CASE-REVIEW.md) are pending exact-version review; their questions remain open. SDD-002 v0.4 and SDD-008 v0.1 retain their exact prior approved scopes; no SDD-013 approval is inferred. SDD-003, 007, 010, 011 and 024 provide related evidence, message, account/linking and privacy boundaries according to their actual statuses. [Engineering Governance §15](../ENGINEERING_GOVERNANCE.md) and D-102 require proportionate alternatives, failure and recovery analysis, verification and visible residual risk. D-098 preserves separate specification and implementation authority.

## Evidence basis and limits

Sources checked 2026-11-13 are technical inputs, not adopted standards, legal conclusions or product selection. [NIST SP 800-63B-4](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html) distinguishes phishing-resistant cryptographic authentication from manually entered OTP, and describes lifecycle and recovery threats. [W3C WebAuthn Level 3](https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/) describes platform and roaming authenticators and user verification; actual MFA depends on the configured authenticator and verification, not the label “passkey.” [RFC 6238](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6238) explains TOTP's shared secret, clock and replay considerations. [W3C accessible authentication guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/) informs cognitive-access evaluation. [OWASP authorization guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html) supports explicit default-deny checks on each request. None establishes an iTakda assurance level, WCAG conformance or control effectiveness. Product-specific compatibility, commercial terms, current support, jurisdictions, processing and prices have not been verified because no products are nominated.

## Internal MFA: concrete candidates

| Candidate and required combination | Benefit and foreseeable burden | Credible failure, mitigation and remaining risk | Selection evidence and cost inputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Roaming WebAuthn security key** individually assigned, with user verification or a separately verified factor sufficient for MFA | Can provide origin-bound phishing resistance and a non-personal-device path; user can move key between compatible workstations. Key purchase, spare issuance, ports/NFC, accessible interaction, enrollment and replacement require operational support. | Lost key, inaccessible touch/PIN, incompatible endpoint or compromised session causes denial or misuse. Evaluate alternate accessible individually assigned method, controlled replacement and session invalidation; origin binding does not fix malicious endpoints or stolen sessions. | Test actual browser/OS/key matrix, user-verification behavior and factor composition, shared-workstation isolation, assistive technologies, loss, and provisioning. Quote initial/spare/replacement equipment, support hours and lifecycle; no current price assumed. |
| **Platform WebAuthn passkey** on individually controlled organization-managed workstation, with user verification where it supplies the second factor | Convenient without a personal phone; potentially phishing-resistant and no separate hardware token for a compatible device. Device ownership, platform access, PIN/biometric accessibility, portability and workstation turnover matter. | Device loss/reimage, inaccessible verification or credential synchronization/recovery account compromise can block or redirect access. Evaluate device-bound versus synchronized credential custody and alternative non-biometric activation; neither a device nor passkey name alone proves two independent factors. | Test actual configuration and factor verification, device replacement and sync account ownership/processing, workstation use, privacy, accessibility and offboarding. Compare device-management, support and potential sync-provider costs. |
| **Password plus individually assigned hardware TOTP token** | Works without personal device and may work without mobile connectivity; readable input and clock maintenance are needed. Hardware cost, seed handling and issuance/replacement are ongoing. | Phishing proxy can relay OTP; theft, duplicated seed, clock drift or replay causes risk. Protect enrollment and seed custody; evaluate guessing/replay controls and safe resynchronization. Training does not make OTP phishing-resistant. | Test clock drift, replay, seed custody, phishing scenario, accessible display/input and lost device recovery; obtain hardware and operational quotes. RFC defaults are not iTakda policy values. |
| **Password plus TOTP on an individually assigned organization-provided device** | Familiar workflow without requiring a personal device, subject to accessible app and device controls. Additional managed device, app maintenance and battery availability increase expense. | Malware, device loss, copied seed or phishing can defeat it; controlled binding and revocation reduce but do not eliminate exposure. | Test accessible entry and screen-reader experience, endpoint compromise, backups/seed transfer and replacement. Compare devices, management and support against hardware keys. |
| **Password plus personal-phone push or SMS** | Familiar for some users; carrier/service, signal, device and privacy dependencies apply. | Push fatigue, device compromise, SIM transfer or delivery failure can deny or misdirect access. It cannot be the sole route under AC-028. Any evaluation needs a secure non-personal-device alternative, independently attributable enrollment and controlled recovery. | Test accessible alternatives, fatigue and number change, outages, loss and actual processing terms; obtain full provider/telecom and support costs. No SMS/provider selection or new communication purpose follows. |

**Conditional direction:** evaluate a roaming cryptographic key and a separately accessible, individually attributable alternative first, with platform passkeys and hardware TOTP as comparators. Do not assume one device meets every access need, or approve a weaker fallback without examining whether it undermines the stronger method. Specialist review must check factor composition, no-personal-device coverage, session protection, enrollment and lifecycle before PO disposition. A key can be one factor or a multi-factor authenticator depending on configuration; hardware ownership by itself is not proof of MFA. Phishing, endpoint compromise, user lockout and insider recovery abuse remain even with a strong primary method.

## Guest proof and linking: evidence of independence is decisive

| Candidate | Trade-off / failure and privacy | Conditional evidence and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Secret issued at booking and separately retained by booker**, presented for one appointment with independently assessed issuance | Convenient account-free path but a copied, observed, phished or lost secret can be used by another person. Accessibility, secure presentation, storage, replay and replacement impose user and support burdens. A bearer secret demonstrates possession, not necessarily original-booker identity. | Map creation, delivery and later use against threat actors and correlated channels. Demonstrate why this is independent original-booker authority under D-103, not a renamed reference/QR or email-possession proof; until PO/security review resolves that question it cannot be treated as compliant. Scope to an appointment and permitted actions, minimize disclosures, and assess replay/abuse and authorized loss recovery. |
| **Two independently established proofs**, e.g. a separately retained booking-time secret plus another independently verified channel or approved evidence | Could reduce reliance on one compromised item, but imposes effort, possible connectivity/device exclusion and more data. Two items sent together to the same mailbox or printed together may fail together and are not automatically independent. | Compare enrollment, source, access and compromise paths; document what each proves, correlated failure, accessibility and permitted message purpose. PO and security/privacy reviewers decide acceptable evidence and independence; no channel or factor is preapproved. |
| **Assigned staff-assisted recovery with independently reviewed evidence** | Allows users who lose proof to seek help; staffing, trained coverage, accessibility and review cost time. Impersonation, collusion and excessive personal data or document collection are risks. | Resolve GUEST-Q01–Q05 and service-specific policy; demonstrate separate permission, independent check, minimal evidence, attribution and escalation. Staff sympathy, matching data or contact possession alone is insufficient; no default identity-document or biometric collection. |
| **Email link, reference, QR, account session or known data alone** | Simpler, but leakage/shared mailboxes and guessing allow unauthorized access and it conflicts with the explicit policy boundary. | Exclude as sole authority. An email confirmation is not independent proof just because a URL or OTP appears in it. D-095 and SDD-007 govern whether any message purpose is allowed. |

For individual linking, authenticated account control plus approved independent proof, explicit confirmation and attributable evidence are needed; no bulk linking or automatic attendee/guardian authority follows. Representative evidence and visibility remain service-specific decisions. Uncertain claims are denied or referred for authorized assistance; avoid telling an unverified requester whether an appointment exists. No proof candidate is demonstrated sufficient by this paper.

## Permissions and contested actions

| Candidate | Benefits, failure and burden | Conditional disposition and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Scoped role grants plus resource checks** | A reviewable matrix can map actor, service, action and installation; ordinary role names simplify administration. Risk: broad role or an omitted object/service check exposes data; matrix changes and revocation require maintenance. | Prefer as the baseline comparison only if every direct operation checks effective scope, missing information denies, grants cannot self-elevate, and changes are attributable. PO must decide actual matrix; test cross-service, cross-installation, object ownership, revoked grants, direct requests and sensitive-action authorization. |
| **Scoped roles plus contextual attribute/policy evaluator** | Can express service-specific relationships and conflicts; adds rule precedence, attribute freshness, operational ownership and audit/debug complexity. | Compare on real authorized cases and failure modes; deny missing/stale inputs and test conflict/precedence. Use only if demonstrated benefits outweigh complexity; no external engine or dependency selected. |
| **Title-based broad administrator access** | Low configuration cost but fails D-108 default-deny and separation of sensitive permissions. | Exclude as an authorization model. A case reviewer, audit viewer or host operator does not automatically obtain reversal, export or client-record authority. |
| **Assigned trained reviewer accepts a contested claim, then places an action/link-specific hold** | Preserves unrelated authorized work and permitted attendance, but coverage, review and escalation need staffing; dishonest claims and mistakes remain risks. | Resolve CASE-Q01–Q06, including acceptance threshold, evidence, assignment, conflicts, release versus separately authorized reversal, unknown outcomes and coverage. Test failed proof alone does not hold, unauthorized reviewers cannot mutate, unrelated access and attendance remain, and no cancellation or false success occurs. |
| **Automatic hold on failed proof or blanket appointment lock** | Easy to trigger, but enables denial of service and contradicts D-103/D-108. | Exclude. The claimant cannot accept, release their hold or reverse a link. Hold status is not successful execution; notices must follow authoritative outcomes and D-095. |

Separate audit viewing from export and distinguish case review from hold release, link reversal and appointment changes. Required evidence should support attributable decisions and authoritative outcomes while withholding proof secrets and unnecessary personal data. Evidence reader, fields, retention, restored-copy restrictions and actual staff obligations await SDD-003/024 and qualified privacy review; no universal event schema is proposed.

## Sole-super-admin recovery alternatives

| Candidate | Risk, workload and constraints | Future evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Client-designated independent authorizer plus controlled recovery executor** | A feasible one-admin model if independent authority exists and can be verified without the recovering admin or a standing operator entitlement. Requires per-installation designation, handover, absence/death/turnover alternatives, training and review. Collusion, forged authority or unavailable authorizer remain. | PO decides authority and substitution; security tests forged/self-approved requests, independent verification, loss, denial when unavailable and restricted execution. Evidence should attribute authorization and outcome without exposing secrets. Invalidate affected sessions/factors as required by the reviewed lifecycle, and check closure. |
| **Pre-positioned independently controlled recovery materials plus independent authorization** | Could shorten recovery but requires secure distribution, custody, rotation and compromise handling. Material held solely by the affected admin does not provide independent authorization; two copies in one custodian do not establish independence. | Demonstrate separate control and action-specific authorization, custody and replacement, single-admin usability and compromise response; no particular recovery code, escrow or approval count is selected. |
| **Standing developer override, shared emergency account, or self-held code alone** | Faster apparent access but conflicts with no standing operator authority, individual attribution and independent sole-admin recovery. | Exclude as sole recovery route. Exceptional support remains explicitly client-authorized, scoped and attributable; it cannot become an unrestricted backdoor. |

Pending, rejected or uncertain recovery does not create a protected session or temporary business access. Enrollment-only/recovery context must not silently become a business session. Additional super-admin appointment requires independent approval and fresh verification with no self-approval; this is distinct from factor replacement.

After separate authorization, verify covered roles cannot reach protected access before successful MFA, accessible no-personal-device pathways, factor enrollment/replacement/revocation and session invalidation, and independent one-admin recovery without backdoors. Exercise correlated guest-proof compromise, reuse and cross-appointment attempts; explicit linking; direct permission bypass, stale grants, self-elevation, unauthorized audit export; scoped hold and independent review, continued attendance and no automatic cancellation. Include accessible keyboard, screen-reader and cognitive journeys, outage and lost-device scenarios, neutral denial and outcome-consistent restricted evidence. These are proposed checks only; there are no test results, approved thresholds, costs, selected providers or accepted residual risks.

Affected implementation remains dependent on applicable exact-version SDD/interface approval, resolved policy questions, qualified specialist review and separate high-risk implementation authorization.

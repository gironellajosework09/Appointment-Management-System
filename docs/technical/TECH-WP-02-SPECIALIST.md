# TECH-WP-02 E — Specialist review handoff

**Preparation only, 2026-11-13.** This is not a legal opinion, privacy impact assessment, security assessment or specialist sign-off. The authoritative [Security review register](../security/SECURITY.md#qualified-philippine-privacy-and-security-review-register) and [SDD-024](../specs/SDD-024-PRIVACY-DATA-LIFECYCLE-AND-INCIDENT-HANDLING-READINESS.md) govern the scope; PH-01–PH-10 remain open. No person is assigned by this draft.

| ID | Exact question and decision affected | Qualified reviewer and smallest evidence needed | Latest boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| PH-01 | Which laws and NPC issuances apply to the actual processing, locations and people? | Philippine privacy counsel, plus local counsel where facts warrant; installation locations, data flows and service description. | Before affected processing/design reliance. |

**Cross-track review questions:** Security/identity and accessibility specialists should assess phishing resistance, individual attribution, accessible non-personal-device MFA, proof independence, representative boundaries, sole-admin independent recovery and no standing operator bypass. Data-integrity and security reviewers should assess booking interleavings, required evidence atomicity, unauthorized audit view/export, secret-free diagnostics and uncertain outcomes. Operations and privacy reviewers should assess notification processing limited to D-095's six purposes, provider ambiguity, incident ownership, backup custody and restored restrictions. Assessments must examine actual proposed implementation and installation facts, not generic vendor claims.

**Sequence:** (1) Engineering prepares the candidate boundary, data-flow and evidence inventory; (2) qualified privacy/legal/security/accessibility and integrity reviewers examine affected decisions in parallel; (3) PO resolves material policy and visible residual-risk questions, and client/provider owners agree installation responsibilities; (4) under separate authority, conduct applicable implementation and verification; (5) independent PH-10 review and release/deployment approvals occur at their required boundary. Unrelated decisions need not wait for every PH item. No legal role, retention period, proof method, specialist finding or risk acceptance is supplied here.

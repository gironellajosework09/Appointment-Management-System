# Evidence and notification consistency — proposed architecture model

> **Draft for review, 2026-11-13.** This is a conceptual model and decision input, not an approved architecture, schema, provider, dependency, implementation scope or operational procedure. No code, database, mail, backup/restore or tests have been run. Governance §5 requires applicable specification approval, bounded implementation authorization and specialist review independently of this document.

## Authority and scope

The model applies to the source-required evidence and six-purpose email boundaries in [SDD-003](../specs/SDD-003-REQUIRED-AUDIT-AND-OPERATIONAL-EVIDENCE.md) and [SDD-007](../specs/SDD-007-SIX-PURPOSE-EMAIL-BOUNDARY-AND-DELIVERY-RECOVERY.md), read with [TECH-WP-02](TECH-WP-02-EVIDENCE.md), [Governance](../ENGINEERING_GOVERNANCE.md) §§5, 11, 15 and 17, D-095/D-098/D-102/D-109/D-110 and the effective [requirements v0.9](../requirements/REQUIREMENTS-SPECIFICATION-v0.9.md), [v0.8](../requirements/REQUIREMENTS-SPECIFICATION-v0.8.md) and unchanged [v0.7](../requirements/REQUIREMENTS_SPECIFICATION.md). SDD-003 v0.4 and SDD-007 v0.2 remain approved only within their exact scopes; v0.5 and v0.3 respectively are pending review. [SDD-006](../specs/SDD-006-SHARED-BOOKING-INTEGRITY-AND-AUTHORITATIVE-OUTCOMES.md) owns booking outcome consistency, while [SDD-024](../specs/SDD-024-PRIVACY-DATA-LIFECYCLE-AND-INCIDENT-HANDLING-READINESS.md), [SDD-025](../specs/SDD-025-BACKUP-RESTORATION-AND-RECOVERABILITY-EVIDENCE.md) and [SDD-026](../specs/SDD-026-DEPLOYMENT-MAINTENANCE-AND-EXCEPTIONAL-SUPPORT-READINESS.md) own lifecycle, restoration and operational responsibilities. This proposal changes no requirement, approval or consumer authority.

## Logical boundaries and responsibilities

| Participant | Input and responsibility | Output / boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Owning feature command | Validates actor, scope, applicable policy and authoritative state; determines whether its action is eligible and what source-required evidence is necessary. | Established success, known rejection or unknown outcome. Owns reconciliation and prevents unsafe repetition; no notification component decides booking authority. |
| Required evidence contract | Receives only approved necessary actor, installation, action, affected scope and outcome context from the owning feature. | Attributable evidence consistent with the established outcome. Separate authorization applies to viewing, export, correction, disposal and custody; no broad administrator or operator entitlement is inferred. |
| Notification eligibility / intent | Applies D-095's purpose-specific trigger, recipient and permitted content after the owning feature establishes the corresponding eligible event. | A proposed durable, recoverable representation of an eligible notification, scoped to that event and installation. It is not a message delivery claim, new command or authorization token. |
| Notification processor and transport | Reads only authorized eligible work, evaluates the applicable recovery decision, and submits permitted content to a separately approved transport. | Evidence-limited processing observations and safe recovery. It cannot repeat the owning business command, change a booking or independently expand recipient access. |
| Diagnostics and authorized operations | Diagnose with separately protected, minimum necessary information; monitor capacity and failed/uncertain processing under reviewed responsibilities. | Neutral public failures and restricted diagnostics; diagnostics do not replace mandatory evidence. Restores, exports and incident actions require separate authority. |

These are conceptual interfaces, not selected modules, APIs, database objects or event payloads. Exact event catalogs, keys, status terms, permissions, retention and custody remain open. Any correlation or deduplication identity must be installation-scoped, protect privacy and be governed by the owning feature; its mechanism and lifetime are not selected.

## Proposed consistency boundary

For an action requiring audit and an eligible transaction notification, the candidate is one local atomic boundary containing the authorized business change, its required audit evidence and a minimal durable notification intent. All three become established together, or none is represented as an established success. This is a **candidate design**, contingent on demonstrably shared transaction/connection and transactional storage; a queue name, ORM callback or after-commit dispatch does not by itself establish atomicity. Evidence needed for rejected or security attempts may have its own reviewed contract and must not be misrepresented as a successful business change. Ordinary diagnostic failure is not automatically equivalent to loss of required audit evidence.

The intent represents eligibility to process a permitted notification; it does not indicate transport submission. The processor runs independently after the authoritative outcome and intent are established. A notification failure never reverses a committed appointment and cannot trigger a repeat booking command. If durable intent cannot be established with the action under a proposed design, that design must demonstrate an equally reliable, reviewed recovery method before affected implementation approval; do not silently treat post-commit best-effort dispatch as sufficient. For account verification, password recovery and guest access, each consuming specification owns its distinct trigger and proof/authority checks; do not assume these are booking transactions or invent a universal commit rule.

| Point in lifecycle | Knowledge and safe behavior |
|---|---|
| Before commit | Validate scope, policy and required evidence/notification eligibility. Do not submit a booking, cancellation or rescheduling success email for uncommitted, rejected, held or authoritatively unknown state. If required evidence or proposed required intent cannot be established within the candidate boundary, do not claim success or leave partial state. |
| Commit acknowledged | Business state, mandatory evidence and eligible intent are established together only if the chosen boundary actually proves it. Present the established accessible on-screen/printable outcome. Processing the notification may begin independently; provider unavailability does not undo the outcome. |
| Commit response lost or uncertain | Browser silence is not authoritative failure. Reconcile the same intent against authoritative state before any unsafe retry. If committed, the eligible notification remains allowed even though the browser did not receive a response; if state remains unknown, do not assert success or independently generate success mail. |
| After commit, before processor starts | Durable eligible work must remain recoverable despite process failure, subject to verified persistence and recovery design. A wake-up signal or after-commit callback can be lost; it is not itself evidence that work is durable. |
| Processor claims and submits | Competing workers and interrupted attempts require reviewed coordination and reconciliation. Establish what is known about the attempt; re-evaluate applicable recipient and content authority according to the consuming contract. Do not assume that a stale recipient or token remains valid. |
| Transport response or later signal | Distinguish local pending/attempted, provider acceptance, explicit failure and unknown where supported by actual evidence; the final vocabulary is undecided. Provider acceptance or a send callback does not prove inbox receipt, reading or identity. Later bounces or conflicting/out-of-order signals need provider-specific interpretation. |
| Timeout or crash around submission | A provider may have accepted a message even when no response was received. Preserve uncertainty; indiscriminate retry or failover can send duplicates. Provider-specific deduplication or reconciliation may reduce risk but cannot be assumed. No exactly-once or inbox-delivery guarantee is made. |

## Six purpose boundaries

The effective v0.8 recipient/content matrix and D-095 control every message; this table is an interface summary, not new policy. [SDD-007](../specs/SDD-007-SIX-PURPOSE-EMAIL-BOUNDARY-AND-DELIVERY-RECOVERY.md) owns the shared contract and the relevant feature owns its event and proof requirements.

| Permitted purpose | Eligibility and minimum recipient/content boundary |
|---|---|
| Account verification | Verification request for the pending account; address being verified; single-purpose content without appointment/attendee details. |
| Password recovery | Account recovery request; the account's verified address; no appointment list, attendee details or credential disclosure; neutral responses. |
| Appointment-scoped guest access | Applicable independent original-booker proof and service-policy checks; approved booking contact and only the authorized appointment/action scope. A link or address alone proves no additional identity or authority. |
| Established booking | Authoritative booking committed; authorized original booker's approved contact, limited to that recipient's permitted view. |
| Established cancellation | Authoritative cancellation committed; currently authorized original-booker contact and permitted cancellation outcome. |
| Established rescheduling | Authoritative rescheduling committed; currently authorized original-booker contact, with new details only where permitted. |

Reminders, marketing, SMS, phone OTP and other purposes remain outside this boundary. A durable work record, queue payload, failed attempt, diagnostic, provider event or backup must not become an unrestricted copy of a message, proof secret or personal record. Qualified review decides the necessary content, retention, processing, notice and access before affected handling; no retention period or provider is selected.

## Recovery, restoration and divergence

Recovery is an authorized operation, not an automatic replay of everything found in an old backup. An isolated restore or test environment must not send real messages or connect to a live transport; production restoration and any re-enablement require separate operational authority and reviewed safeguards. Restore business state, mandatory evidence, notification intent/attempt observations and their access restrictions as a consistent scope where feasible, then identify the backup point and any gap relative to the last known operational state. A restored record may describe an earlier state than a provider or user has already observed. Missing later audit evidence or business changes, and already-sent or uncertain notifications, cannot be repaired by assuming they never happened.

Before resuming processing, authorized owners reconcile available authoritative state and permitted external evidence for each affected work item: whether the business action was established, whether its recipient and purpose remain authorized, and whether a submission is known, failed or uncertain. Where evidence is insufficient, retain uncertainty and refer the affected work for the reviewed recovery process; do not silently mark delivered, blindly resend, recreate an appointment, or delete contrary evidence. Correction or reconciliation remains attributable and preserves prior history under the applicable consumer and evidence contracts. Exact handling of unrecoverable gaps, external divergence, retained token validity and customer communication requires PO and specialist decisions; no new notification purpose is created here.

Access and privacy restrictions apply to restored copies, archives, failed-job data and diagnostic artifacts. Verify capacity and integrity before reliance; diagnostic rotation cannot dispose of required audit. SDD-025 requires protected backups and a tested restore procedure before operational reliance, while a procedure test is distinct from verification of an actual client's backup. No restore, retention duration, recovery objective or deletion guarantee is authorized or claimed.

## Alternatives and review decisions

| Alternative | Benefit and material trade-off | Decision/evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Local coordinated business/evidence/intent transaction | Strong candidate for consistency and recoverable work, with fewer cross-system commit gaps; couples command availability to required local persistence and adds write/storage/operations costs. | Engineering and data-integrity/security reviewers verify boundaries, durability, capacity and failures; PO decides architecture after review. |
| After-commit dispatch without durable intent | Simpler initial processing and avoids dispatch before commit, but a crash between commit and dispatch can lose work. | Engineering must show an equally reliable reconciliation path and its privacy/cost burden; after-commit ordering alone is insufficient evidence. |
| Separate queue or remote evidence store | Can separate workload/custody, but introduces cross-system gaps, credentials, provider costs and recovery complexity. | Assess atomicity/reconciliation, provider terms and restricted data processing; PO decides material service selection with qualified reviews. |
| Reconstruct from authoritative records | May avoid an intent representation but requires sufficient history to distinguish unsent from uncertain/already sent, and can expose stale recipients or data. | Demonstrate complete eligibility and attempt evidence, safe recovery and retention impact for each feature; do not assume reconstruction is possible. |

The Product Owner resolves material architecture and risk trade-offs, scope and provider decisions. Engineering owns consumer contracts and failure design; security/data-integrity and qualified privacy reviewers assess tamper, access, threat and processing implications. Actual client/provider/operations owners must be assigned for monitoring, evidence custody, transport, incident response and restore before reliance. EVD-Q01–Q04 and MAIL-Q01–Q04, provider capabilities, event identity and deduplication lifetime, stale-recipient handling, authorized reader/export scopes and restore divergence decisions remain open. No residual risk is accepted here.

## Proposed verification, subject to separate authorization

1. Exercise commit, rejection and evidence/intent write failure at each boundary, including capacity exhaustion, rollback and lost response; establish no partial state, false success or unsafe duplicate command, and reconcile authoritative unknown outcomes.
2. Crash or interrupt between commit, wake-up, claim, submission and acknowledgement; test competing workers, timeout, provider outage/throttle, ambiguous acceptance, duplicate and out-of-order signals, failed work and recovery. Measure what the chosen mechanism actually proves, including its duplicate-message limits.
3. Verify each of the six purpose/recipient/content contracts and each consumer's trigger/proof integration, including held/rejected outcomes and browser-response loss. Ensure restricted attendee details, credentials and proof secrets do not leak through evidence, jobs, logs or status.
4. Verify server-side view/export separation, direct and cross-installation denial, neutral public failures, authorized correction provenance, diagnostic capacity/rotation and protected access to restored copies.
5. In an authorized isolated environment, restore a deliberately older state and exercise divergent provider observations, disabled outbound mail, unresolved gaps, custody and controlled re-enablement. Record actual evidence and limitations; a test double cannot establish real-world delivery or actual-client backup recoverability. Apply applicable automated and human keyboard, screen-reader, zoom and touch checks to user-facing status and recovery.

These are proposed criteria, not results. No implementation, operational, delivery, accessibility, privacy/security or recovery verification or acceptance is claimed.

# Targeted architecture evidence E — qualified-review handoff

**Preparation only, 2026-11-13.** The detailed [PH-01–PH-10 matrix](TECH-WP-02-SPECIALIST.md) remains the source for each exact question, qualification and milestone; it is not repeated as a new privacy review. D-111 requests parallel handoffs. No specialist has reviewed or approved these models.

| Review / qualifications | Specific decision-ready questions and required evidence | Boundary and decision authority |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy/legal: qualified Philippine privacy counsel and practitioner; other jurisdiction counsel where actual facts require | PH-01–04: which jurisdictions, actual controller/processor roles, impact assessment scope, purposes, notices and legal bases apply? Supply actual installation locations, proposed category/flow and provider terms; no roles or bases inferred. | Qualified reviewers advise; PO/client resolve product/contract scope with legal advice before affected processing/provider adoption. |
| Privacy/legal and client operations | PH-05–07: how verify subject/representative requests, minimize proof and sensitive fields, assign visibility, and manage category-specific retention, backup and restored restrictions? Supply representative scenarios, evidence fields, data lifecycle and access matrix. | Counsel/practitioner advise; PO/client determine permitted policy and actual roles before affected collection/handling. |
| Incident and provider: qualified privacy/legal counsel, security incident lead, client/provider operators | PH-08–09: who preserves evidence, escalates and determines reporting; where do host/mail/subprocessors process data, who can access it, and what are exit and incident terms? Supply actual contracts, flow, support access and incident scenarios. | Qualified legal review and actual responsible owners before provider adoption and operational reliance; PO decides material provider choice. |
| Independent privacy/security assessors | PH-10: do implemented controls and actual evidence support release, and are findings dispositioned? Supply approved scope, threat model, implementation, tests, operations and risk register. | Independent review and disposition before release as required; no approval inferred from this handoff. |
| Identity security and accessibility specialists | Can organization-provided cryptographic keys meet actual MFA composition and individually attributable non-personal-device use? Is alternate/recovery equivalently controlled? Is guest proof independent under correlated-channel compromise? Supply device matrix, issuance/loss, actor permissions and representative scenarios. | Security/accessibility reviewers assess; PO decides material factor, proof and authority choices before affected implementation. |
| Data-integrity/security specialists | Do all conflict domains, absent rows, policy revisions and old/new allocations participate in the same authoritative outcome? Do timeout, lost commit response, same-intent replay, audit and intent failure preserve invariants? Supply proposed model, selected-version docs and future synthetic test oracle. | Review before affected implementation approval; PO decides material trade-offs and explicit risk disposition. |
| Security/privacy/operations specialists | Are evidence view/export distinct, logs minimized, delivery ambiguity honest, and restored restrictions and worker responsibilities enforceable? Supply event/data flow, six-purpose matrix, queue failure and restore scenarios, access matrix and provider terms. | Review before affected processing and operational reliance; actual client/provider owners allocate duties. |

**Sequencing:** Engineering produces bounded data flows and candidates; qualified reviewers assess independent areas in parallel; reconcile shared guest-proof, audit, restore and installation assumptions; PO decides policy/mechanisms and actual owners agree duties. Separately authorized implementation and synthetic verification precede independent PH-10 assessment and release where required. A particular real-backup verification requires distinct authorization and privacy controls under D-091. Unrelated evidence work does not wait on every PH question.

## Purpose and scope

Enable individually accountable internal users to authenticate and perform only the client-installation, service and action work they are explicitly authorized to perform. Authentication answers who is acting; permission evaluation answers what that actor may do here. Neither host custody nor seeing a control grants business authority.

**In scope:** proposed installation-local first-super-admin and internal-user provisioning, internal staff/administrator sessions and mandatory MFA coverage for every authenticated internal user (including operators only when separately granted internal access), controlled factor lifecycle/recovery, server-side permissions, safe failure and attributable identity/permission/recovery evidence.

**Out of scope:** guest proof/management (SDD-008), customer registration/account lifecycle (SDD-010), individual appointment linking (SDD-011), actual case-review procedures (SDD-013), operational credential/host/client-installation provisioning or support procedures (SDD-026), business commands, physical identity schema, vendor/tool selection and implementation. No public internal-user signup or universal role catalog is introduced.

D-047's staff/supervisor/administrator boundaries are source roles, not a completed permission matrix. Apply permissions by actor, action and service, with default denial. No user may grant themselves authority; sensitive actions require separately authorized approval under the approved policy. The term first-super-admin denotes the highest-privilege application administrator within one installation, not a cross-client master identity or operator entitlement. Appointing an additional super-admin requires independently authorized approval, fresh verification, no self-approval and attributable evidence. Sole-super-admin recovery must have independently authorized handling and no unrestricted bypass. The mechanism, initial custodian, exact permission assignments and operational procedure remain open. Reusable default/shared administrator credentials are prohibited. No factor/vendor/package, recovery protocol or numeric timeout/lockout value is approved.

### Bounded installation-local super-admin — proposed authority

The super-admin is the proposed highest **application-level** administrator in one independent client installation, not a cross-installation owner or infrastructure master identity. Powers depend on reviewed server-side permissions and applicable business/security rules; the title supplies no bypass.

| Candidate administrative responsibility | Permission and safety boundary |
|---|---|
| Provision/manage internal users | Only within authorized installation/action scope and the controlled identity/MFA/evidence lifecycle. No public privileged signup, shared/default credentials or automatic operator administrator. |
| Assign/revoke approved roles/permissions | Only the reviewed grant/revoke/delegation authority; no invented complete role matrix. Self-elevation, delegation of highest privilege and changing security-sensitive assignments need explicit material-policy review before affected implementation. |
| Manage governed client business configuration | Routine services, schedules, capacity and staff within approved authority; server-side validation, audit and applicable booking-integrity/existing-appointment checks still apply. The developer's initial setup does not create a permanent business-data entitlement. |
| Perform explicitly authorized administrative actions | Only their approved action/service/data scope. Supervisor exceptions, trained assigned case review, evidence access and high-risk changes retain their own permission/review boundaries; a super-admin label does not grant all of them. |

A super-admin cannot automatically bypass MFA, protected business rules or independently controlled security/recovery safeguards; silently delete/conceal/alter mandatory evidence; perform undocumented impersonation; access other client installations; or give technical operators unrestricted business authority. No impersonation feature is introduced: any future proposal needs its own scope, explicit authority and evidence controls. Appointing an additional super-admin requires a controlled process with separate authorization, strong fresh verification and attributable audit evidence; an ordinary unrestricted role assignment is insufficient. How authorization remains independent when only one super-admin exists is unresolved. High-impact delegation, self-grant/revocation, privileged recovery and sensitive-action approval/segregation remain unresolved; no role matrix or unrestricted emergency power is selected.

### Proposed first-super-admin and MFA lifecycle

1. Before provisioning, identify the explicitly authorized client/installation custodian, target installation and provisioning authority through reviewed controls. Confirm that no earlier first-admin identity/attempt is being silently replaced; uncertain completion requires authoritative review, not blind recreation.
2. The developer/installer may initiate the reviewed provisioning process under explicit client/setup authority, but the intended client custodian establishes its own individually controlled credentials and MFA. Bind the identity to that installation and reviewed highest-privilege scope, with unique controlled activation/enrollment. No reusable/default/shared admin credential, public privileged self-provisioning, copied client credentials or automatic permanent operator entitlement. Mechanism/channel is unselected; the developer must not silently retain client credentials or super-admin access.
3. Permit only the necessary reviewed bootstrap/enrollment context while proof and MFA enrollment are incomplete; do not expose client records or general administration as a bootstrap shortcut.
4. Establish the required MFA, record consequential provisioning/enrollment/permission outcomes consistently with SDD-003, and activate protected internal access only when required checks/outcome/evidence are established. Failure or uncertainty never manufactures an active super-admin or duplicate principal.
5. Subsequent internal users receive only explicit reviewed assignments. At handover, review/revoke or separately authorize any remaining temporary setup application access and record its outcome; no silent retained developer credentials or permanent super-admin access. Operational installation/host credentials and detailed handover/support procedures remain SDD-026; this contract authorizes no execution.

**Proposed mandatory coverage:** every authenticated internal staff member, supervisor, administrator and super-admin must meet MFA at internal sign-in; an operator separately granted internal application access must meet the same baseline plus applicable privilege controls. This does not cover customer/guest authentication (SDD-008/010), choose factors for infrastructure credentials (SDD-026), or authorize operator access. Enrollment/recovery cannot be relabelled as a permanent password-only exception.

**Controlled lifecycle:** factor enrollment, replacement, revocation and recovery require reviewed identity/permission checks, safe pending/rejected/unknown outcomes and attributable evidence. Revoked/replaced factors must not silently retain the access they formerly supplied under the reviewed lifecycle. Factor secrets, recovery codes and credentials must not enter audit/diagnostic output. Exact assurance, session invalidation, replay/loss handling, notification purposes and transition rules remain open; no new D-095 email purpose is created.

**Super-admin recovery:** use a stronger, separately controlled authorization/review path than routine staff recovery, capable of serving an installation with **only one super-admin**. Do not assume a second super-admin exists or make recovery depend on one by default. Preserve independent identity verification, separately controlled authorization, individual accountability, required evidence, replacement/revocation of lost or compromised factors and appropriate session invalidation under the reviewed lifecycle. Pending/rejected/unknown outcomes cannot grant recovered authority or prompt blind repeat provision/reset. Exact custodians, evidence, assurance and invalidation protocol remain open.

Neither ordinary staff, a claimant, a current session, email possession nor operator/host custody alone may silently replace highest-privilege factors. A reviewed second administrator may be an additional option, not the sole recovery model. Independent verification/authorization can require separately established client authority or reviewed assistance without assigning a new universal role here. No final protocol, custodian arrangement, email-only shortcut, permanent password-only exception or unrestricted emergency bypass is selected.

**Staff without personal devices:** support alternatives that are separately assessed and approved for assurance, accessibility, custody and recovery (for example organization-managed authentication capability). No factor is approved by this example. Lack/loss of a personal device must not force device ownership or silently disable MFA. Alternative access remains individual and attributable; no shared account/factor shortcut.

### Factor evaluation and risk-based fresh verification

Engineering/security must compare TOTP authenticator applications, passkeys/security keys with suitable phishing-resistant capabilities, and accessible individually attributable alternatives for staff without personal devices. TOTP is an evaluation candidate, not the selected/default factor. Review compatibility with the chosen stack, maintenance/licensing, assurance/threat exposure, enrollment/replacement/revocation/recovery, device/custody/accessibility needs and operating cost. These evaluation subjects make no vendor/package, factor implementation or compatibility claim. No email-OTP shortcut or permanent password-only bypass is approved.

Propose MFA at internal sign-in and additional fresh verification for appropriately classified high-risk actions, such as reviewed highest-privilege delegation, factor/recovery changes or separately controlled security decisions. These are candidate classifications for review, not final trigger policy. Routine appointment work should not automatically demand MFA at every click/interaction; server-side authorization/state checks still apply at every protected decision. Exact session lifetimes, inactivity/freshness thresholds, trigger lists and invalidation/race behavior need engineering/security assessment and Product Owner review of material policy. Fresh authentication does not establish the outcome of an earlier business command.

### Developer-led setup and client-managed business administration

The preliminary delivery model is one maintained complete reusable product, with an independent application/database per client. The developer performs initial technical and business configuration only within reviewed client/setup authority and initiates controlled first-super-admin provisioning. The client establishes and controls its own individual credentials/MFA; the configured installation is handed over with required identity/permission/setup evidence.

After handover, authorized client administrators manage routine services, schedules, capacity and staff within reviewed permissions. Technical upgrades, maintenance, operational credentials and exceptional support follow separate authorized arrangements under SDD-026; actual custodian allocations/procedures are not selected here. Routine client business administration does not mean only client-managed hosting: D-083's client-managed and provider-managed hosting constraints remain. No retained unrestricted developer/operator business-data access, cross-client administrator or support backdoor follows from installation.

For any exceptional client-record access, the proposed safeguards require applicable explicit client authorization, scoped permissions, bounded duration, MFA, attribution, review and closure. Operational credential/authentication methods remain for SDD-026 design; this does not select them or grant such access. SDD-002 governs application identity/permissions; SDD-026 owns operational credentials, installation/handover, maintenance and support procedures. These proposed implications do not authorize configuration functions or operational execution in this task.

Session/credential storage, secure request handling, rotation/invalidation, accessible factor/recovery use and abuse prevention require assessment with the authorized stack and Security. No MFA package, vendor, factor, recovery mechanism or compatibility claim is selected. Accessible feedback must support keyboard/focus/assistive technology and non-personal-device paths without exposing secrets or relying on hover/color alone.

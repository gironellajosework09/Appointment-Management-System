For a defined candidate and installation profile, gather identifiable evidence against applicable approved requirements, feature criteria and dependencies, including version, environment/profile, owner, method, date and result where actually available. Missing, stale, simulated or unexecuted evidence is clearly identified; a draft, plan, demo or approval is not a test result. Trace cross-path identity and service/action denial, guest/account boundaries, booking and same-intent integrity, evidence/notification outcomes, data isolation, privacy and operational recovery to their owning features. Core workflows require applicable automated success, rejection, authorization and failure-path tests before release under NFR-010. D-070/NFR-008's 50 simultaneous valid contenders for one remaining unit must establish exactly one success and safe outcomes with no overcapacity, duplicate-rule, partial-state or orphaned violation; daily volume is not concurrency and this is not a latency promise.
